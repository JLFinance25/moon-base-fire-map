The problem

Moon-base air burns differently than Earth air

The challenge page says a Moon base will likely hold air with 30% oxygen or more at 10.2 psi or less. More oxygen makes materials much more flammable.

NASA has run fire tests on the space station for years. Which of them ran in air like that?

Who this helps

Students and the public, who can see the gap at a glance. Mission designers, who can see where planned Moon-base conditions go beyond what past space fire tests covered.

Limits

  • Small liquid droplets, not a cabin fire
  • Only 24 tests at 30%+ oxygen
  • FLEX only, not a multi-experiment dashboard
  • Gas mixes and fuels differ across tests
